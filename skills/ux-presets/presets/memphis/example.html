Parents ask

Do they need to know any code?

No. Every week starts from zero, and older kids who have coded before get harder challenges.

Do they bring a laptop?

No, we provide laptops and robot kits. They take their project home on a link.

What about lunch?

Bring a packed lunch. We have fruit and water all day.
